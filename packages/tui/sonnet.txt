The golden hours slip through our grasp,
Like sand through fingers, fleeting and light.
Each moment's gift we must not mishap,
But cherish well this brief and shining flight.

The dawn brings hope, the dusk brings rest,
In every sunrise, promise renewed.
Though time moves fast, we must be blessed
To witness beauty, like flowers emerged.

So let us walk where wisdom shines,
With grateful hearts and minds open wide,
And in these verses, truths align—
Life's simple joys are where love resides.

Thus write I this, that you may see
The beauty that in moments lies.
